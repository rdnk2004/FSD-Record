Franz Beckenbauer

Franz Beckenbauer, legendary German footballer and manager, famously known as "Der Kaiser" (The Emperor).

Timeline of Der Kaiser's Life

  • 1945 - Born on September 11 in Munich, Germany.
  • 1964 - Made his professional debut for FC Bayern Munich.
  • 1965 - Made his debut for the West German national team.
  • 1972 - Captained West Germany to victory in the European Championship and won the Ballon d'Or.
  • 1974 - Captained West Germany to win the FIFA World Cup.
  • 1976 - Won the Ballon d'Or for the second time.
  • 1977 - Joined the New York Cosmos in the United States to play alongside Pelé.
  • 1990 - Managed West Germany to win the FIFA World Cup as their coach.
  • 2006 - Led the organizing committee for the 2006 FIFA World Cup in Germany.
  • 2024 - Passed away on January 7 at the age of 78.
