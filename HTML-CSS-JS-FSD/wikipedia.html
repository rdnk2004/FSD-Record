Franz Beckenbauer

Beckenbauer in 1974

Franz Beckenbauer (11 September 1945 – 7 January 2024) was a German professional footballer and manager. Nicknamed Der Kaiser ("The Emperor"), he is widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the history of football.[1] He is one of only three men to win the FIFA World Cup as both a player and a manager.[2]

Early Life

Beckenbauer was born in the ruins of post-war Munich. He grew up in the working-class district of Giesing and started playing football at the age of nine.[3] He joined the youth academy of FC Bayern Munich in 1959.

Club Career

Beckenbauer made his debut with Bayern Munich in 1964. During his time at the club, he helped them win four national league titles and three consecutive European Cups from 1974 to 1976.[4]

International Career

He earned 103 caps for West Germany, playing in three FIFA World Cups. He captained the national team to victory in the 1974 World Cup by defeating the Netherlands in the final.[2]

Managerial Career

As a manager, he led West Germany to the World Cup final in 1986, and later won the tournament in 1990.[5]
